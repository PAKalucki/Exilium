The independent title index is recorded in [the test fixture](../tests/fixtures/sw2015-options.json). Its coverage test requires every book title to be either selectable or explicitly excluded, without overlaps or unexplained extras. This establishes title coverage, not a complete certification of every mechanical interaction.

Pomysłowy Dobromir's full entry on printed page 38 requires Zdolności nadprzyrodzone (Niesamowita nauka), the associated skill, and constructs devices using powers and Power Points. Złota rączka on page 39 also requires Niesamowita nauka. Their short table rows omit those details. The user confirmed supernatural options should stay excluded. McGyver is the selectable mundane improvisational inventor option.

The other excluded Edges are Czarodziej, Mentalista, Mnich-Wojownik, Nowa moc, Odporny na moc, Odporniejszy na moc, Odzyskiwanie mocy, Regeneracja mocy, Przypływ mocy, Punkty mocy, Wojownik Światła/Ciemności, Wybraniec, Wyssanie Duszy and Zdolności nadprzyrodzone. Szósty zmysł and Władca zwierząt retain the initial policy of awaiting a specifically mundane table adaptation. Niedowiarek is the excluded Hindrance because its drawback depends on actual supernatural phenomena.

Step 3 now includes accent-insensitive title search and readable reasons for omitted options, including ordinary alternatives where appropriate. These informational entries cannot be selected. The audit also corrected Błyskawiczny refleks to require Zręczność k8, as stated in the full entry; existing character choices are not silently rewritten.

## Beginner guidance and the setting guide

The creator and sheet offer native, keyboard/touch-accessible question-mark popups. They explain the five attributes, how they differ from skills, linked-skill costs, dice/Wild Die/raises, derived stats, conditions, Fuksy and advancement. Each attribute also has a visible short description in step 2. Sheet entries display the fuller Edge/Hindrance explanations already used in the creator.

Pochodzenie, Status społeczny and Profesja use explicit dropdowns with a custom-entry option. The old native datalist filtered suggestions to the existing text and obscured other choices. The 12 origin choices include all five described major cities, the five described smaller towns, plus generic Pustkowia/Karawana backgrounds. The five castes and location descriptions are paraphrased from the supplied Exilium guide; custom entries are preserved. Selected descriptions appear below the control in both creation and later profile editing.

These are narrative choices: selecting an origin, profession or caste does not grant traits, skills, Edges or money. The shared poor-start ruling and separate approval warnings for wealth Edges remain in effect. No magic, alien character species or automatic cybernetic bonuses are added.
