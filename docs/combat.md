# Combat tracker — Savage Worlds PL 2015

Open **Walka** to prepare a named encounter. MG and Administrator manage it; signed-in Players read a shared view that refreshes every three seconds while the page is visible. Add finalized, unarchived character sheets. Each sheet appears once: use separate copies of an Extra for opponents that need independent wounds. A sheet can participate in only one active encounter at a time. Reinforcements added during combat receive initiative next round.

Every Figura has its own initiative unit. Add Blotki to that unit for player-controlled allies, or form NPC groups. The first member represents the group for initiative Edges. Set groups and surprise before combat. An ambusher begins on Hold; a surprised unit receives a first-round card only with an entered Notice total of at least 4. Normal dealing resumes next round. The GM enters final test totals after modifiers; the app does not roll trait dice.

## Cards and turns

- A persistent 54-card deck has two Jokers. Deal each round; rank descends Ace to Two, with Spades, Hearts, Diamonds, Clubs breaking ties. Cards are not returned after every ordinary round. Reshuffle after a round in which a Joker was drawn. If the draw pile runs out, recycle only available discards. Held cards and unused Tactician reserves never enter the shuffle.
- Błyskawiczny refleks (Quick) redraws a card of 5 or lower until a higher card appears. Opanowany (Level Headed) draws two cards and Wyjątkowo opanowany draws three; choose the best before applying Quick. A group uses its representative's Edges once.
- A Joker is ready to act at any moment, including interrupting without an opposed roll. Its +2 applies to trait and damage results only in the round it was dealt. Keeping that card on Hold into a later round does not extend its bonus. Ordinary Jokers do not grant extra Fuksy in this implementation; no optional Joker's Wild setting rule is enabled.
- Finish the current turn, or put it on Hold. Held actions carry their card across rounds and receive no replacement. Use the between-actions control only between declared actions. To interrupt a declared action, enter opposed Agility totals: a winner acts first and the interrupted turn resumes; a loser acts after the current action. A tie marks both actions simultaneous and preserves the second action even if its actor becomes incapacitated. Resolve their actual effects simultaneously at the table.
- A held, Shaken unit must act after another turn. Incapacitated participants are skipped; a group still acts if any member can act. After all ordinary turns have finished or gone on Hold, deal the next round. Finish an encounter to retain it as read-only history and release its sheets for another encounter.

One **Taktyk** can enter a Knowledge (Strategy) total before combat. Success and each raise reserve one card outside the initiative deck. At each round's start the GM either gives one reserve card to an ally or explicitly skips the exchange before turns begin. The card can replace a whole unit's card, or detach selected Blotki from their Figura/group for independent initiative. Detached allies rejoin next round after acting; if still on Hold they retain their separate card until they act. Unused reserves remain outside all reshuffles until the encounter ends.

## Conditions and linked sheets

Rany, Zmęczenie, Szok, Wyeliminowanie and personal Fuksy use the actual linked character sheet, including its revision and audit trail. Combat and sheet writes commit together. A stale encounter or sheet returns a conflict; refreshing reads the latest state. Retrying an unconfirmed request reuses its command ID, so damage or a spent Benny is not duplicated. Starting or finishing combat does not reset a sheet's conditions or personal Fuksy.

- **Shaken:** enter a modified Spirit total once at the start of that participant's turn. The supplied 2015 book uses an ordinary success of 4 to recover and act normally; a raise is not required. Failure leaves only free actions, including Pace movement without running. A personal Benny, or an eligible NPC's GM-pool Benny, removes Shaken at any time. The panel reminds the GM about wound/fatigue penalties, Combat Reflexes and a current Joker. Leadership range and other situational modifiers remain table decisions.
- **Damage:** enter final physical damage and effective Toughness after armor, AP, location and other modifiers. A success causes Shaken; another damage-caused Shaken adds one wound. Each raise instead inflicts one wound, without an extra wound for already being Shaken. Enter wounds prevented by Soak and choose the spent Benny's pool before applying the result. A failed Soak can spend a Benny with zero wounds prevented. Soaking every wound from that hit also clears earlier Shaken.
- **Wounds:** a Figura can carry three wounds and is incapacitated only by wounds beyond three; its stored wound counter stays capped at three. An Extra's first wound incapacitates it. Incapacitation, injury-table results, bleeding out, healing, death and special attacks are adjudicated at the table; corrections use the ordinary sheet counters and flags. Fatigue's third level also incapacitates.
- **GM Fuksy:** enter the remaining session pool at encounter setup, optionally using one per participating Player as the session-start suggestion. Extras have no personal pool. GM Figury use their sheet's personal pool as well; the 2015 session-start allowance is two, which the GM sets on that sheet. The tracker does not silently replenish personal pools or assume that a new encounter starts a new session.
- **Maneuvers:** Prone, Defense, Full Defense and Desperate Attack are encounter markers. Prone sets melee Parry to 2 and reminds about Fighting −2 and the movement cost to stand. Defense gives Parry +2. Full Defense uses the entered Fighting +2 total, never below normal Parry. Desperate Attack reminds about Fighting/melee damage +2 and applies Parry −2. Defense markers expire when the next turn begins; Prone stays until cleared. Other temporary modifiers can be recorded in a combat note. These markers do not rewrite a permanent build or invent extra character-sheet fields.

The damage form resolves a physical hit after table rolls; use Shaken controls directly for tricks, fear and other sources that do not cause the repeated-damage-Shaken wound. It does not automate attack rolls, movement, multi-action declarations, targeting, encumbrance-dependent choices, healing or the injury tables. Source: the supplied Polish 2015 book, especially printed pages 29, 33, 35, 69–81 and 108–109; no Adventure Edition Distracted/Vulnerable states are added.

## Privacy, storage and recovery

Character privacy is applied on the server to combat page/API data. Cards, initiative groups and turn progress are shared. Private statistics, conditions and combat notes remain hidden from other Players and by default from Admin. Owners and MG see full details. Admin must explicitly reveal private participants before managing the encounter; the reveal also applies to its add-character chooser. Future shuffled deck order is never sent to any browser, and reserves/draw history are restricted to an authorized manager. Disabling an account or changing its role takes effect on subsequent reads and writes.

Encounters and audited commands live in SQLite schema migration 6. The existing snapshot includes them; restore accepts schema 6 and still invalidates sessions and tokens. Completed encounters preserve their initiative and command history, while condition displays continue to read the current linked sheets. Combat endpoints require a browser session; personal API tokens cannot read or write encounters.

Tests cover rank/suit ordering, initiative Edges, card conservation across 200 rounds, Hold/Joker retention, surprise, Tactician reserves and detached allies, interrupt wins/losses/ties, damage and Soak, 2015 Shaken recovery, access/redaction, live linked-sheet reads, idempotency, revision conflicts, rollback and backup/restore. Browser workflows exercise the shared panel, role/privacy rules, retries and responsive widths.
